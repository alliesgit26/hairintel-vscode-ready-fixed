Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2026

HairIntel AI provides digital consultation, client profile, extension planning, and AI-generated hairstyle preview tools for hairstylists and salons. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared with service providers, and how users can contact us about privacy questions.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, email address, and account information submitted by users.
  • Subscription and payment status information connected to Stripe checkout.
  • Client consultation details entered into the platform, such as current hair length, desired length, density, texture, hair goals, scalp sensitivity, color goals, and extension preferences.
  • Images uploaded or generated for AI hairstyle preview purposes.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, usage activity, and error logs.
  • Support messages or information submitted when users contact us.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide HairIntel AI software features and consultation tools.
  • Generate hair extension recommendations and AI hairstyle previews.
  • Create and manage user subscriptions.
  • Process payments, subscription updates, cancellations, and refunds.
  • Store client consultation records when users choose to save them.
  • Improve app performance, security, reliability, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or misuse of the platform.

3. Payment Information

HairIntel AI uses Stripe to process subscriptions and payments. We do not store full credit card numbers or full payment card details on our servers. Stripe may collect and process payment information according to its own privacy and security practices.

4. AI Image and Consultation Data

HairIntel AI may use third-party artificial intelligence services to generate hairstyle previews or related outputs. Information submitted for AI previews, including uploaded images and style preferences, may be processed by those AI service providers to deliver the requested feature.

Users should not upload images or client information unless they have permission to use that information in the platform.

5. Service Providers

We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help us operate the platform. These may include payment processors, hosting providers, database providers, authentication tools, analytics tools, email/support tools, and AI service providers.

Examples of service providers may include:

  • Stripe for payment processing and subscription management.
  • Supabase for database and account-related storage.
  • Vercel for app hosting and deployment.
  • OpenAI, Stability AI, or similar providers for AI-powered features.

6. How We Protect Information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain subscriptions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and operate the platform. Users may contact us to request deletion of account or client profile information, subject to legal, security, and payment-record retention requirements.

8. Children’s Privacy

HairIntel AI is intended for hairstylists, salons, and adult business users. It is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. User Choices and Requests

Users may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with their account, subject to applicable law and necessary business or legal retention requirements.

10. Third-Party Links

The platform may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
