Request account deletion

HairIntel lets you permanently delete your account through a secure, verified sign-in. This process deletes the HairIntel authentication account and the account/subscription records associated with it. Consultation information stored only on your device is cleared from that device as part of the deletion flow.

Before deleting an account with an active subscription

Subscription cancellation and account deletion are separate. If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel or manage the subscription in Google Play to stop future renewals. If you subscribed on the HairIntel website, HairIntel will attempt to cancel the connected Stripe subscription during account deletion.

To continue:

  • Open the secure deletion flow below.
  • Sign in to the HairIntel account you want deleted.
  • Review the billing notice and type DELETE to confirm.
