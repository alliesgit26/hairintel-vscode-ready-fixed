Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

HairIntel AI provides digital consultation, client profile, extension-planning, placement, safety, and AI-generated hairstyle preview tools for hairstylists and salons. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared with service providers, and how users can exercise account and privacy choices.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, email address, authentication identifiers, and account information submitted by users.
  • Subscription status, plan, entitlement, transaction identifiers, and purchase-verification information from Google Play or Stripe. HairIntel does not receive full payment-card numbers from these billing providers.
  • Client consultation details entered into the platform, such as current hair length, desired length, density, texture, hair goals, scalp sensitivity, color goals, and extension preferences.
  • Images uploaded or generated for AI hairstyle preview purposes.
  • Technical information such as browser or device type, IP address, usage activity, app/version information, and error logs when available.
  • Support messages or information submitted when users contact us.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide HairIntel AI software features and consultation tools.
  • Generate hair-extension planning guidance and AI hairstyle previews.
  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Verify subscription purchases, maintain paid-feature entitlements, process subscription changes, and prevent payment fraud.
  • Store client consultation records when a feature explicitly saves them to HairIntel's services; some consultation data may instead remain stored locally on the user's device or browser.
  • Improve app performance, security, reliability, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or misuse of the platform.

3. Payments and Subscriptions

Android in-app subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. HairIntel receives purchase and subscription status information needed to verify entitlement but does not receive full Google Play payment-card details.

Website subscriptions may be processed through Stripe. HairIntel does not store full credit-card numbers or full payment-card details submitted to Stripe. Google Play and Stripe process payment information under their own privacy and security practices.

4. AI Image and Consultation Data

HairIntel AI may use third-party artificial intelligence services to generate hairstyle previews or related outputs. Information submitted for AI previews, including uploaded images and style preferences, may be processed by those AI service providers to deliver the requested feature.

Users should not upload images or client information unless they have permission to use that information in the platform.

5. Service Providers

We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the platform. These may include payment and app-store providers, hosting providers, database providers, authentication tools, analytics/error tools, support services, and AI service providers.

Examples may include Google Play for Android subscriptions, Stripe for website billing, Supabase for account/database services, Vercel for hosting and server functions, and AI providers used for requested AI features.

6. How We Protect Information

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Purchase entitlement is verified through server-side services rather than relying only on client-side subscription state. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain and verify subscriptions, comply with legal or tax obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and operate the platform. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law or by a payment provider even after an account-deletion request.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users can initiate permanent HairIntel account deletion from account settings inside HairIntel or from the public account deletion page. The secure deletion flow requires sign-in to verify ownership of the account before deletion.

When the authenticated deletion succeeds, HairIntel deletes the authentication account and the current server-side profile and subscription records associated with that account. HairIntel also clears HairIntel consultation/profile data stored locally in that browser or app instance. Data that must be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute, or payment-processing obligations may be retained only for the applicable required period.

Google Play subscription cancellation is separate from HairIntel account deletion. Android subscribers can use Google Play subscription management to stop future renewals. Website subscriptions connected to Stripe are canceled by HairIntel during account deletion when the connected Stripe subscription can be identified.

9. Children’s Privacy

HairIntel AI is intended for hairstylists, salons, and adult business users. It is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 as account holders.

10. User Choices and Requests

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with their account, subject to applicable law and necessary retention requirements. Account deletion can be initiated through this page. For other privacy requests, contact HairIntel AI Support.

11. Third-Party Links

The platform may link to third-party websites or services, including Google Play subscription management. HairIntel is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
